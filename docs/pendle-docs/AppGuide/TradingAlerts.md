# Trading Alerts

Trading Alerts send you Telegram messages about your positions and about Pendle.

You can turn on alerts for:
- Limit order fills
- Looping liquidation risk
- New market launches
- Pendle newsfeed

## Guides

1. Connect your wallet and sign in.
2. Click the **bell** icon at the top of the app.
3. Click **Link**, then click **Open**. Telegram opens the Pendle notification bot.
4. In Telegram, press **Start**. The link code is valid for 10 minutes.
5. Go back to the app. The panel shows **Telegram linked**. Turn on the alerts that you want.

For more alert settings, click **Manage alerts** in the panel.

## Privacy {#privacy}

Pendle runs public Telegram groups. Thus, we keep your alerts account separate from your activity in these groups.

**We store:**
- Your wallet address.
- Your numeric Telegram user ID. We need this ID to send you messages.
- The time that you linked your wallet.

**We do not store:**
- Your Telegram username, name or profile photo.
- Your phone number. Telegram does not give phone numbers to bots.
- The messages that you send in Pendle groups.

**We do not:**
- Match your alerts account to your membership in Pendle community groups.
- Share your Telegram ID with third parties.
- Use your Telegram ID for marketing.

The Pendle app itself does not receive your Telegram ID. It only knows if your wallet is linked or not linked.

## Stay safe

- Start the link only from the bell icon in the Pendle app. Do not use bot links from other people.
- The bot never asks for your seed phrase, your private key or a wallet signature.
- Pendle team members never send you a direct message first.

## Unlink

To remove the link between your wallet and your Telegram account, send `/unlink all` to the bot. This deletes the link for all of your wallets.
