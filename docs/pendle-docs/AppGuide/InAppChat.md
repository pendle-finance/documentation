# In-app Chat

Get help directly in the Pendle app. Click the **Peepo** icon at the bottom right of any page to open the chat.

## AI

Ask Pendle AI anything about Pendle: market data, strategies that match your goals, or how to use the app. Answers are instant.

## Support Tickets

Talk to a Pendle moderator.

1. Open the chat and click the **Ticket** tab.
2. Click **Create a support ticket**.
3. Add a title and describe what happened. You can attach screenshots.
4. Click **Submit ticket**.

Your ticket opens as a chat. A Pendle moderator replies there, and you can send more messages and files. When your issue is solved, click **Mark resolved**.

You do not need to connect a wallet. However, if you do not connect a wallet, your tickets are only saved in that browser. Connect your wallet to see your tickets on all your devices.

## Priority Chat

Users who hold $1M or more on Pendle unlock [Priority](https://app.pendle.finance/priority). Priority users get a **Pendle Team** tab in the chat, for direct chat with the Priority desk.

Priority also includes:
- A weekly report in the Priority hub
- Personal insights on what you hold
- A named Pendle contact

Check your progress on the [Priority page](https://app.pendle.finance/priority).

## Stay safe

- The Pendle team and moderators never ask for your private key, your seed phrase or wallet approvals.
- Only ask for help in the Pendle app. Do not trust people who send you a direct message first.
